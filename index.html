What stays yours

The model talks. Your rules decide.

Lumi is fluent, but it is never trusted with money or safety. These four checks run on the server on every order.

Prices come from your menu

The total is recomputed on the server from your price list and modifiers. A total the model makes up never reaches checkout.

Only what you sell

Every item is matched to your menu. Off-menu or sold-out items are refused, and the customer is offered what you do have.

Allergens are blocked, not guessed

A declared allergy blocks items tagged with it. Where you haven't published allergen information, Lumi says so instead of reassuring.

Nothing is ordered unconfirmed

The customer sees the cart, the total and dine-in · takeaway, and confirms. "Place the order" alone is not a yes.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Does it connect to my POS?

Not yet. During the pilot, orders arrive in the Lumi merchant console with a chime, and your staff key them in or prepare them from there.

What happens to my customers' data?

It is yours. Lumi only processes it to run your ordering. A customer can ask for their data to be deleted, and it is.

What does it cost?

The pilot is a fixed fee for six weeks, credited if you continue. We go through it on the call, with your outlet count in hand.

Will it replace my staff?

No. It takes the order and the questions in the queue, then hands off to a person for anything it shouldn't answer.
